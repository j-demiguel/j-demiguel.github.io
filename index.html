About

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at King's College London (Human-Centered Computing Group and Marketing Department), funded by the Faculty of Natural, Mathematical & Engineering Sciences. I am also a permanent visitor at Nokia Bell Labs, Cambridge.

My research focuses on sociotechnical safety (AI incidents, AI alignment) and the future of workers (responsible AI governance in organisations and its impact on work). I am lucky to have Prof Daniele Quercia and Dr Andrés Gvirtz as my supervisors. My industry mentors are Dr Sanja Šćepanović and Dr Edyta Bogucka.

Before the PhD, I was a Research Assistant at The Alan Turing Institute, working on the gender gap in AI startups and venture capital, and how this impacts innovation. I also worked at the London School of Economics, analysing NHS data for targeted care policy. I hold an MSc in Applied Social Data Science from LSE and a BS in Philosophy, Politics and Economics from the University of Manchester, with an Erasmus+ year at Sciences Po, Paris.

My work has had impact at the national level, being featured in The Guardian, and I have also worked in policy with think tanks, such as Avanza, discussing the EU regulation of social media, big tech, and AI. I also share academic topics with the wider public and other sectors, at the Círculo de Bellas Artes (Spain) and the Congress of Argentina.
